## Responsibility

Solves problem 8: *too lost in the market — no visibility into trends or a game plan*.

- Trending skills and roles in the user's target area
- Demand signals for their target stack and domain
- Cross-reference with Career Memory: where you stand vs. the market
- Skill gap roadmap: prioritized list of what to learn next
- Game plan output: 30/60/90 day strategy based on profile and goals

## Owns

Market data and derived strategy output. Not the profile.

## Why Phase 4, and why High complexity

Needs an external market data source that hasn't been identified yet, and is only useful cross-referenced against a mature Career Memory profile. The highest-complexity module in the product, and the one with the least certain data foundation.

## Status

Not startable. No market data source has been chosen.
